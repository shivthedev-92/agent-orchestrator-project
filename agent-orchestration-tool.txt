Problem Statement :

    While agents are gaining power in transforming the Application and Automation Scenario in current IT 
    people do not have an explicit idea of what it is or how to work with it. With Orchestra.AI, people 
    will be able to orchestrate agents by choosing from variety of models instead of a single model, and people could focus on 
    using the ai by customizing it based on skills and adding attributes to their agents. This idea is not just for developers
    but for anyone with workflow. You could build and orchestrate agents for building a data pipeline, or you could build and 
    orchestrate an agent for planning your trip to los angeles. 

Plans: 

    - Orchestra.AI - a studio for agents to work togather regardless their background. they can be from open ai or claude, 
    they will be able to relax context and messages to each other on their work. 
    - the initial design view is already created in the folder - /home/shivthedev/projects/agent-orchestrator-project/Agent Orchestration App
    - I am thinking to build this app with React in Frontend, python  or go backend, with a good DB. I am also open to tech stack choice here, since 
    I have not built a project like this.
